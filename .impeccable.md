## Design Context

The context below comes from the PRD, the approved phone mockup, and `public/static/style.css`.
Brian did not answer the teach questions in a live session. Ask him before a change to the direction.

### Users

The managers of one fantasy football league, the Cornerstone league. They read the site on a phone,
mostly. They read the weekly page after the Tuesday publish, and they grade a trade offer on the analyzer page.

### Brand Personality

Unconfirmed, Brian to confirm: Sports print: bold, blunt, league-insider. The weekly page reads like a sports section, Unconfirmed, Brian to confirm: with banter.
The analyzer must feel quick and sure, and the grade must land with some drama.

### Aesthetic Direction

- Black, red, and white. A black masthead, white pages, grey cards, one red accent.
- Helvetica Neue, uppercase labels with wide tracking, heavy numbers with tabular figures.
- Light scheme only. The brand keeps the same colors in a dark system theme.
- One centered 480px column at every width.
- Unconfirmed, Brian to confirm: Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards, gradients for decoration, soft pastel cards.

### Design Tokens

The one source for token names and values is `src/app/globals.css`.

### Design Principles

1. Same look as the Jinja pages. A reader cannot tell the React page from the old one.
2. Phone first at 390 wide. No horizontal scroll, ever.
3. Motion confirms an action. Only transform and opacity animate, with a strong ease-out. Reduced motion turns movement off.
   Exception: the team picker collapse animates height. The list is short and the collapse is rare. A FLIP of the whole form below would move the long player lists.
4. Numbers are the stars: heavy weight, tabular figures, big sizes.
5. No hex color and no pixel size outside the tokens.
